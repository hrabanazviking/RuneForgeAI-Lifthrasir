## Safety principles

- LLMs generate hypotheses and proposed changes, **not privileged authority**.
- Read-only and offline modes first; risky changes require approval and recovery.
- Lab verification is separate from live deployment; no unverified kernel hotpatching.
- Federated research is opt-in and cannot remotely control participating hosts.
- Contributions to the Linux kernel go through the real maintainers with legitimate **human** DCO sign-off.
